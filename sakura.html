TEAM 7 • KONOHAGAKURE

SAKURA HARUNO

Sakura Haruno is a kunoichi of Konoha who develops from a young Team 7 member into a highly capable medical ninja and close-combat specialist. Her growth comes through disciplined training, precise chakra control and a strong commitment to protecting others.

CHARACTER STORY

BACKGROUND & HISTORY

Sakura begins her career as a member of Team 7 with Naruto and Sasuke. Realizing that she needs more strength and practical skill, she trains under Tsunade and becomes a powerful medical ninja. Her advanced chakra control allows her to deliver extraordinary physical attacks while also performing complex healing and emergency treatment on the battlefield.

KEY ACHIEVEMENTS

LEGACY

  • Helped protect and treat wounded shinobi in major battles.
  • Continued Tsunade's medical-ninja legacy in Konoha.

CHARACTER IDENTITY

WHAT DEFINES SAKURA?

Sakura is defined by growth, discipline and her ability to combine intelligence with medical expertise and immense physical strength. Her progress is built through training rather than inherited power alone.
