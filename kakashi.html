KAKASHI HATAKE

Kakashi Hatake is an elite shinobi of Konoha known as the Copy Ninja. As Team 7's leader, he guides Naruto, Sasuke and Sakura while teaching them that teamwork, judgment and protecting comrades are essential to a shinobi's life.

CHARACTER STORY

BACKGROUND & HISTORY

Kakashi's early life is shaped by his father Sakumo Hatake, his teammates Obito Uchiha and Rin Nohara, and the experience of the Third Shinobi World War. He later becomes a jonin instructor and leads Team 7. His experience, calm judgment and understanding of loss make him an important mentor to Naruto, Sasuke and Sakura. After the Fourth Shinobi World War, he serves as the Sixth Hokage.

KEY ACHIEVEMENTS

LEGACY

  • Earned the reputation of the Copy Ninja.
  • Led Team 7 through some of its most important missions.
  • Developed and mastered the Lightning Blade.
  • Served as a major battlefield leader during the Fourth Shinobi World War.
  • Became the Sixth Hokage after the war.

CHARACTER IDENTITY

WHAT DEFINES KAKASHI?

Kakashi is defined by experience, composure and a strong belief in teamwork. His past losses shape his determination to protect his students and prevent them from repeating the mistakes of earlier generations.
